# Code of Conduct

This project expects respectful, technically serious collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Critique ideas, code, and tradeoffs without attacking people.
- Assume good intent, but be precise when something is wrong.
- Keep discussions grounded in evidence, reproducible behavior, or clear reasoning.
- Help maintainers and contributors keep the project useful to the next person reading the thread.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- discriminatory or demeaning language
- repeated bad-faith argument or deliberate derailment
- doxxing, threats, or unwanted contact
- spam, trolling, or hostility toward maintainers or contributors

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this policy.
They may also restrict participation for repeated or serious violations.

If you need to report conduct problems privately, use `contact@flow-like.com` and include links or screenshots when possible.
